# Pip — purchasing, with perspective

## Design decision

An editorial purchasing studio: warm paper #f4f1e9, ink #20231f, electric cobalt #2449ef, vermilion #f6623e and measured chartreuse #d9ed82. Bricolage Grotesque provides display character; DM Sans keeps operational reading clear. Strong typographic scale, thin rules, generous asymmetric space and tactile supply objects replace repeated faint panels.

## Composition

The first desktop viewport contains a large authored headline, one primary purchasing action and an original interactive supply sculpture. The sculpture fills its area and connects three real locations to current cover/capacity values. A distinct three-part operating strip below routes into stock signals, supplier changes and orders. Existing detailed stock/budget/activity content follows in the same material language.

Scenarios are case studies: a numbered index, specific quantity comparisons, then an actual recorded-decision receipt. Inventory visualizes true stock cover on a common ruler and true demand observations. Approvals are purchase documents with recorded original/proposed quantities when available. Utility routes share the same display font, ink, controls and section rhythm.

## Motion story

First entry reveals headline lines, sculpture and the primary action in a short stagger. Pointer movement gently changes the sculpture view; selecting a labelled location changes its scene state and corresponding current-data readout. Scenario selection swaps its diagram; returned decisions arrive as a receipt. Dropdowns and disclosures have short purposeful transitions. No simulated progress, invented savings or fake live transactions. No scroll hijacking. Reduced-motion users see the composed final state.

## Research

Awwwards website and Site of the Day galleries; Terminal Industries (logistics interaction and product-world relationship); Lusion / its designer’s case study (ownable 3D identity and integration); Obys Grids / Obys portfolio (editorial hierarchy and structured layouts); Exo Ape (typographic scale and cinematic framing). Exact sources and observed limitations will be recorded in REHEARSAL.md after review. These are composition references, not assets to copy.

## Review targets

Desktop 1280/1440/wide composition, short-height access, coherent all-route tokens, readable small text, native keyboard and editing behavior, real functional flows, reduced motion, no layout overflow, disposal and WebGL fallback. Compare full compositions, not isolated buttons.
